What This Course is All About

Video is everywhere, and it's an incredible way to share stories and information! This class is all about giving you the fundamental knowledge and skills to create engaging digital video projects. We'll explore the tools, techniques, and processes used in digital video production through hands-on projects. Your final video project will take you through all five stages: Development, Pre-Production, Production, Post-production, and Distribution.

Here's a sneak peek at some of the cool stuff we'll cover:

  • The fascinating History of Video Production
  • Awesome Storytelling Strategies
  • Getting to know your Basic Production tools, techniques, and equipment
  • Making your videos sound and look great with Basic Sound, Graphics, and Visual Effects
  • Exploring different Production Environments: Studio, Field, and Synthetic
  • Mastering Editing software & techniques (especially Adobe Creative Cloud Software!)
  • And even a look into Digital Media Careers!

Pro-Tip: If you've got some basic knowledge of Adobe Photoshop or Illustrator, you'll be ahead of the game, as they're super helpful in the Adobe Creative Suite workflow!

Learning Outcomes

Here's are the five student learning outcomes for this course

  • Develop clear and coherent planning documentation for their digital video projects, demonstrating an understanding of pre-production processes.
  • Create engaging and coherent digital audio and video sequences, applying principles of storytelling and aesthetic design.
  • Effectively utilize the basic features and tools of industry-standard video editing software (e.g., Adobe Premiere Pro) to complete video projects.
  • Efficiently digitize, organize, and manage audio and video materials for post-production, adhering to best practices for digital asset management.
  • Exploring different Production Environments: Studio, Field, and Synthetic
  • Demonstrate proficiency in recording high-quality audio and video using various equipment and techniques, considering diverse shooting environments.
